## 🎨 PRODUCTION TIPS

### Recommended Tools
- **Screen Recording**: OBS Studio, Camtasia, ScreenFlow
- **Video Editing**: DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro
- **Voice Over**: Audacity for recording, quality microphone
- **Background Music**: Epidemic Sound, Artlist (royalty-free music, low volume)

### Visual Techniques
1. **Zoom and highlights**: Zoom in on important elements
2. **Annotations**: Add arrows and text to guide attention
3. **Smooth transitions**: Avoid abrupt cuts
4. **Visible cursor**: Show where you click
5. **Speed**: Slightly speed up repetitive parts (x1.2-1.5)

### Audio Tips
- Speak clearly at moderate pace
- Pause between parts
- Background music at 15-20% of voice volume
- Avoid ambient noise

### Final Video Structure
```
00:00 - Intro + Logo
00:30 - Homepage
01:30 - Main features
03:30 - Recruitment system
05:00 - Collaborative management
05:45 - Administration
06:45 - Security
07:15 - Tech stack
07:45 - API
08:15 - Bonus features
08:45 - Conclusion
```
